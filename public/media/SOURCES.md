# Internal exercise photo references

Prepared 19 October 2026 for the user-requested local/internal preview.

This is a source record, not a blanket reuse licence. The user requested good
publicly accessible photos for a site that will remain internal. No account,
paywall, subscription or access controls were bypassed. Keep this version out
of public deployment output.

## Source status

- **Your Move**: goblet squat comes from the free-clip selection. The retired thigh-supported row clip is retained locally but is no longer displayed. The
  source permits in-app/site use and editing, with optional attribution.
  Standalone redistribution/resale as a video library is prohibited.
- **U.S. Marine Corps / DVIDS**: the split squat clip is identified as public
  domain on its source page. Credit and the required non-endorsement notice
  appear on the card. See https://www.dvidshub.net/about/copyright.
- **Exercise photo references**: publicly accessible pairs retrieved from
  yuhonas/free-exercise-db. The repository licence must not be treated as
  proof of image reuse rights. Image provenance/rights remain unconfirmed,
  as acknowledged at https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/issues/2.
- **Physitrack / PhysioTools** and **Physio Dynamik**: frames extracted from
  freely viewable public demonstration pages. These works remain copyrighted;
  no republication licence is claimed. They are internal reference assets.
  Original visible copyright marks/branding are retained.

Images are real photographs or extracted video stills. No poses were generated.
Stills were resized to WebP; the split squat frames were also cropped around the
performer. Starting photos are reused for return-to-start captions in the
three-position sequences derived from two-photo references. Captions are our
own, and retain the plan's written instructions and training prescription.
