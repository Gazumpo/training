Exercise guidance and sources

The gym supports sport practice: loadable leg strength for cycling, pushing strength, complementary core work and light shoulder conditioning. Extra pulling and calves are optional. Other slot exercises are alternatives. Diagrams are simplified; use the written instructions as well.

Cycling strength-training review: heavier resistance training can improve cycling performance, but evidence certainty is low and the best dose remains uncertain. This once-weekly dumbbell workout is a practical adaptation, not a directly tested programme. Progress load when recovered; no automatic weight increases.

Climber core-training trial: dynamic and isometric training improved some core and climbing-specific tests, but the study was small and did not establish higher bouldering grades. Dead bugs and side planks are practical choices, not a directly tested prescription.

Climber shoulder-conditioning trial: fewer shoulder injuries in young male amateurs following a broader programme. The exact routine here has not been tested for injury prevention. Serratus muscle-activation study supports the muscle target of the push-up plus movement; muscle activation alone does not prove prevention. Wrist extensions are optional capacity work, not a generic treatment for elbow pain. Climbing injury-prevention review: conditioning, load management and technique are reasonable targets; no short accessory routine guarantees prevention. Protect recovery and respond to persistent symptoms.
